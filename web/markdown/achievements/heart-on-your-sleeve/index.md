# Heart On Your Sleeve (GitHub achievement)

- Status: Disabled (tested, not earnable now)
- How to earn: React to something on GitHub with a ❤️ emoji
- Tiers: ? · 16 · 128 · ?
- Progress in GitVerse: earned or not

Heart On Your Sleeve was an experimental achievement celebrating positive community interactions through emoji reactions. GitHub switched it off to prevent automated reaction spam; existing earners retain their badge on profile pages.

| Tier | Needed |
|---|---|
| x1 · base | unknown |
| x2 · bronze | 16 ❤️ reactions |
| x3 · silver | 128 ❤️ reactions |
| x4 · gold | unknown |

## FAQ

### Can you still earn the Heart On Your Sleeve badge?

No. GitHub concluded testing for this achievement and deactivated it. Profiles that earned it previously keep it on display.

### What were the known milestone thresholds for Heart On Your Sleeve?

Verified community records document 16 ❤️ reactions for Tier 2 (bronze) and 128 for Tier 3 (silver).
